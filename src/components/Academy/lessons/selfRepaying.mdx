### Why an untouched balance only falls

Two forces move an ordinary loan: interest pushing the balance up, and your
payments pushing it down. Alchemix runs on one of them. Redemptions repay your
balance from your own collateral, so a loan you never touch can only fall.

### What moves it back up

You do. Borrowing more is the one thing that raises what you owe. Repaying lowers
it, and you can repay any amount whenever you like, with alUSD, MYT, or USDC.

### What the position card shows

The card tells you how much of your deposit is free to withdraw right now, and
every repayment lifts that figure immediately. The intermediate track works out
the arithmetic behind it.
