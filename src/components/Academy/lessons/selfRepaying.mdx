### Left alone

No interest is added for time passing, and no price move changes what you owe.
The protocol repays the balance out of the position, so an untouched loan only
falls.

### Your own choices

Borrowing more raises the balance. Repaying lowers it, and you can repay part or
all at any time with alUSD, MYT, or USDC.

### What the app shows

The position card shows what is free to withdraw. Repaying raises that figure
straight away. The intermediate track works out the arithmetic behind it.
