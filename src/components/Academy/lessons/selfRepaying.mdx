### Why the balance never rises on its own

Two things move an ordinary loan: interest adding to the balance, and payments
taking it off. Alchemix only has the second. Redemptions repay your balance out of
your own collateral, so a loan you never touch is only ever paid down.

### Where the repayments come from

Savers buy alUSD below face value and deposit it into the Transmuter, where it
redeems for a full dollar after a set term. That queue is what funds redemptions.
As their alUSD matures, the protocol **earmarks** a matching slice of the open
loans, meaning it reserves the collateral behind that slice for the claim ahead.
The earmarked collateral stays in the vault earning until the claim settles, and
settling it repays your debt. Lesson 6 takes the saver's side of the same event.

The **Redemption Rate** on your vault is the pace this runs at, and it moves with
how much alUSD is waiting in the queue against how much debt is in the system.

### What moves it back up

You do. Borrowing more is the one thing that raises what you owe. Repaying lowers
it, and you can repay any amount whenever you like from the Repay tab, which lists
the assets you can repay with.

### What the position card shows

The card shows how much of your deposit is free to withdraw right now, and
every repayment raises that figure immediately. The deposit itself falls as
redemptions spend it on your debt, and what is yours, the deposit less the debt,
holds steady apart from the yield and a small redemption fee. The intermediate track works out
the arithmetic behind it.
