### Why an untouched balance only falls

No interest is added for time passing, and no price move changes what you owe.
The protocol repays the balance out of the position itself, so a loan you leave
alone only falls.

### What moves it back up

Borrowing more raises the balance. Repaying lowers it, and you can repay part or
all of it at any time with alUSD, MYT, or USDC.

### What the position card shows

The card shows what is free to withdraw, and repaying raises that figure straight
away. The intermediate track works out the arithmetic behind it.
