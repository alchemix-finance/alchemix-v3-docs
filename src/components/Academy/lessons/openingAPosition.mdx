A deposit does not sit in the vault as the asset you put in. It is wrapped into the
Mix-Yield Token, which starts earning from the moment it is minted, and your claim
on the whole arrangement is represented by a position NFT held in your wallet.

Borrowing against it mints new alAssets to you at face value. Inside Alchemix, 1
alAsset always cancels exactly 1 unit of debt, whatever the open market thinks it
is worth.

### The cap works in both directions

You may borrow up to **90%** of your collateral's face value. Reaching that ceiling
stops further borrowing and nothing else: the position stays open, the collateral
keeps earning, and nothing is sold.

Read the other way, the same rule is your withdrawal limit. Any collateral not
needed to hold the remaining debt under the cap is free to leave at any time. That
is why a position with 10,000 deposited and 5,000 borrowed does not have 5,000
available to withdraw. The 5,000 of debt needs about 5,556 of collateral standing
behind it at the cap, leaving roughly 4,444.

Both numbers are shown in the vault interface, so nothing here has to be worked out
by hand. Knowing where they come from is what stops the smaller one being a
surprise.
