<Beside />

Say you have had your loan for a month and want to pay part of it back yourself.

<Term id="redemption">Redemptions</Term> pay the loan down over time, and you can also repay it yourself at any time, in part or in full. Repaying lowers what you owe and frees more of your deposit to withdraw.

<There /> is a practice copy of the app's Dashboard, the page that lists what you hold. Your deposit and your loan are one position, shown as a <Stat name="dash-card">card</Stat> under <Stat name="dash-heading">Open Vault Positions</Stat>.

To repay or withdraw, press <Stat name="manage">Manage</Stat> on the card. <Stat name="manage">Manage</Stat> opens the vault page, where Repay and Withdraw are tabs.

<VaultRowFigure lit={["vault", "action", "deposit", "debt", "available", "net"]} />

<PageBreak title="Withdrawing while you owe" />

<Beside />

While you owe anything, part of your deposit has to stay in the vault. Withdrawing shrinks your deposit while your debt stays the same, so your <Term id="ltv">LTV</Term> rises.

A withdrawal can take your LTV up to 90% and no further. With 5,000 owed, about 5,555.56 of your 10,000 deposit has to stay, so you can withdraw 4,444.44.

The <Stat name="tab-withdraw">Withdraw</Stat> tab shows that 4,444.44 as <Stat name="available">Available</Stat> under the field.

<Work title="How the 4,444.44 is worked out">

5,000 divided by 0.9 is 5,555.56, the smallest deposit that keeps your LTV at 90%. 10,000 minus 5,555.56 is 4,444.44.

</Work>

You can try the limit here before withdrawing anything. Type an amount in the <Stat name="field">field</Stat>, and the <Stat name="deposit">Deposit</Stat> and <Stat name="ltv">LTV</Stat> stats preview where that withdrawal would leave you.

<Stat name="max-button">MAX</Stat> fills the whole 4,444.44, which takes LTV to exactly 90%.

<Stat name="action">Withdraw</Stat> is switched off here. You withdraw your whole deposit once the loan is repaid.

<PageBreak title="Repay part of the loan" />

<Beside />

Repaying part of the loan yourself lowers what you owe right away. The practice vault is open on its <Stat name="tab-repay">Repay</Stat> tab, with a <Stat name="debt">Debt</Stat> of 5,000, of which 208 is <Stat name="earmarked">Earmarked</Stat> for the next <Term id="redemption">redemption</Term>.

The <Stat name="tab-repay">Repay</Stat> tab takes <Term id="alusd">alUSD</Term>, USDC or <Term id="myt">MYT</Term>. You repay 1,000 of the loan from the USDC in your <Stat name="wallet">practice wallet</Stat>.

<Do moment="repay-part" title="Repay 1,000 USDC">

With USDC in the <Stat name="token">token picker</Stat>, type 1,000 in the <Stat name="field">field</Stat> and press <Stat name="action">Repay</Stat>.

</Do>

<RepayPartMoment />

Your 1,000 USDC paid off 1,000 of your loan. The repayment cleared the 208 of earmarked debt first, which cost a small fee.

<Changes>
<Change stat="debt">

<Stat name="debt">Debt</Stat> fell from 5,000 to 4,000.

</Change>
<Change stat="earmarked">

<Stat name="earmarked">Earmarked</Stat> reads 0. The first 208 of your 1,000 cleared the earmarked debt, and the other 792 came off the rest.

</Change>
<Change stat="deposit">

<Stat name="deposit">Deposit</Stat> fell by 0.52, to 9,999.48. The 0.52 is the 0.25% <Term id="redemptionFee">redemption fee</Term> on the 208 of earmarked debt, taken as in a redemption.

</Change>
</Changes>

From here on, the stats are worked out from a deposit of 9,999.48, which is why they no longer come out round.

<ReadOn moment="read-repaid" title="What the repayment changed" />

Owing less gives your position more room. With 4,000 owed against almost the same deposit, <Stat name="ltv">LTV</Stat> fell to 40%, and <Stat name="borrowable">Borrowable</Stat> rose by about 1,000.

Repaying also lets you withdraw more. For each dollar you owe, about 1.11 of your deposit has to stay in the vault, so each dollar you repay frees about 1.11.

Your 1,000 repayment raised what you can withdraw by more than 1,000, from 4,444.44 to 5,555.04.

<Work title="How the 5,555.04 is worked out">

1 divided by 0.9 is about 1.11, the deposit that has to stay for each dollar you owe. With 4,000 owed, 4,444.44 of your 9,999.48 deposit has to stay, and 9,999.48 minus 4,444.44 is 5,555.04.

</Work>

<ReadOn moment="read-freed" title="Repaying frees deposit" />

Which part of your debt a repayment clears depends on the token you repay with. USDC and MYT clear the earmarked part first and then the rest, while alUSD clears only debt that is not earmarked.

<Versus
  heads={["USDC or MYT", "alUSD"]}
  rows={[["Clears earmarked debt", "Yes, first", "No"], ["Clears the rest of the debt", "Yes", "Yes"], ["Fee", "0.25% of the earmarked part", "None"]]}
/>

Your USDC cleared the earmarked 208 first, which is why your deposit paid the 0.52 fee.

<ReadOn moment="read-tokens" title="Which token clears what" />

Earmarked debt can be repaid only in MYT, because the MYT you repay with goes to the <Term id="transmuter">Transmuter</Term> to pay Fixed Yield depositors. When you repay with USDC, the app deposits it into MYT for you in the same transaction, so USDC clears earmarked debt too.

alUSD repays at face value, one alUSD for each USDC of debt. Since alUSD typically trades a little under a dollar, repaying debt that is not earmarked with alUSD can cost less than with USDC.

<PageBreak title="Repay the rest and withdraw" />

<Beside />

To withdraw your whole deposit, you first have to owe nothing. Your <Stat name="debt">Debt</Stat> is 4,000, and you pay it off from the USDC in your <Stat name="wallet">practice wallet</Stat>.

On the <Stat name="tab-repay">Repay</Stat> tab, <Stat name="max-button">MAX</Stat> fills either what you owe or what your wallet holds, whichever is smaller.

<Do moment="repay-rest" title="Repay the remaining 4,000">

Press <Stat name="max-button">MAX</Stat>, then press <Stat name="action">Repay</Stat>.

</Do>

<RepayRestThenWithdrawMoment />

Your loan is paid off, and <Stat name="debt">Debt</Stat> reads 0. With nothing owed, none of your deposit has to stay in the vault, so you can withdraw all of it.

<Stat name="available">Available</Stat> on the <Stat name="tab-withdraw">Withdraw</Stat> tab shows your whole deposit, 9,999.48. The deposit is 0.52 under 10,000 because of the fee on the <Term id="earmarked">earmarked</Term> debt you repaid.

<Do moment="withdraw-all" title="Withdraw your whole deposit">

Press <Stat name="max-button">MAX</Stat>, then <Stat name="action">Withdraw</Stat>.

</Do>

<Await moment="withdraw-all" hint="Press MAX, then Withdraw" />

The vault sent your whole deposit, 9,999.48 USDC, to your <Stat name="wallet">practice wallet</Stat>, which closes the position.
