Redemptions clear debt. As Transmuter positions mature, the protocol converts
earmarked collateral into the asset needed to repay debt, then spreads that
repayment across the system in proportion to what each position owes. Each
redemption takes the same value off both sides of your position. That is
deleveraging: the debt shrinks and so does the collateral behind it, and because
the two are like-kind it costs you only the redemption fee.

### Where the rate comes from

Nobody sets the redemption rate. It is worked out from three figures:

- **The alAssets waiting in the Transmuter.** The queue turns over once per term.
- **The term.** A 26-week term turns the queue over twice a year.
- **The total debt in the market.** A year of repayments is compared against it.

> Redemption rate = (queue × terms in a year) ÷ total debt

1,400,000 alUSD on a 26-week term repays 2,800,000 in a year. Against 4,000,000 of
debt that is 70%. The rate rises when savers deposit more or the term shortens, and
falls when borrowers take on more debt. It can run above 100%, which means the
average loan clears inside a year.

Every position in the market sits on the same rate. Owe four times as much and
four times as much gets repaid for you each year, which leaves the same share of
each loan outstanding. The size of a loan does not enter the pace, and neither
does the vault's yield, which raises what your MYT is worth.

Your vault prints the current rate as **Redemption Rate**. It is a projection
that assumes the queue, the term and the debt stay where they are today, so read
it again before you plan around any pace.

### What earmarking pays you

As each saver's alUSD matures in the Transmuter, the protocol earmarks a matching
slice of the open loans, each sized to its share of total system debt. The earmarked collateral
stays in the vault until the claim settles, earning yield throughout. This is **temporal leverage**: the longer the gap between earmark and
settlement, the more yield that collateral collects for you.

alUSD cannot repay an earmarked slice. The Repay tab offers the assets that can.

The vault's Earmarking and Redemptions panels show both sides of this: what has
been earmarked per day over the last month, and what is scheduled to mature next.

### Repaying by hand

Redemptions set the pace for a position you leave alone. Repay part or all of the
debt whenever you like and you free collateral sooner.

The Choosing an LTV lesson works out the exposure a high LTV creates while the
position stays open.
