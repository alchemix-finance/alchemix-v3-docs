Debt in Alchemix is cleared by redemptions. As Transmuter positions mature,
redemptions convert earmarked collateral into the asset needed to repay debt, and
that repayment is applied across the system in proportion to each position's share
of it.

The redemption rate is the share of total system debt that redemptions repay in a
year. It is a property of the protocol, and the same rate applies to everyone in
the market. A position holding four times the debt has four times as much repaid
per year, which leaves both positions at the same point on the curve.

So the size of your loan does not decide how long it takes. The redemption rate
does, and you do not control it.

### What borrowing more does cost

If borrowing more does not cost you time, and there is no interest rate, it is fair
to ask what it does cost.

Two things, neither of them time:

- **Redemption fee** – Charged on the amount redeemed, so it scales with how much
  you borrowed.
- **alAsset discount** – Applies if you sell your borrowed alUSD or alETH below face
  value. Borrow 5,000 alUSD and sell it at 0.99, and you have paid roughly 50 for the
  liquidity up front. The dApp shows the current price before you confirm.

There is a third consideration, which is what a high LTV does to your exposure while
the position is open. That is the subject of lesson 3.

The pace above describes what happens if you do nothing at all. You remain in
control throughout: repay part or all of your debt by hand at any time to unlock your
collateral sooner.
