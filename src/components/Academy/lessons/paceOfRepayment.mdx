Debt in Alchemix is cleared by redemptions. As Transmuter positions mature,
redemptions convert earmarked collateral into the asset needed to repay debt, and
that repayment is applied across the system in proportion to each position’s share
of it.

The redemption rate is the share of total system debt that redemptions repay in a
year. It is a protocol-level parameter, and the same rate applies to every position
in the market. A position holding four times the debt has four times as much repaid
per year, and both positions sit at the same point on the curve.

The size of your loan does not decide how long it takes to clear. The redemption
rate does, and you do not control it.

### What borrowing more does cost

Borrowing more does not cost you time, and there is no interest rate. It carries
two costs:

- **Redemption fee** – Charged on the amount redeemed, so it scales with how much
  you borrowed.
- **alAsset discount** – Applies if you sell your borrowed alUSD or alETH below face
  value. Borrow 5,000 alUSD and sell it at 0.99, and you have paid roughly 50 for the
  liquidity up front. The dApp shows the current price before you confirm.

The exposure a high LTV creates while the position is open is covered in Choosing
an LTV.

The pace above is what happens if you do nothing. You can repay part or all of the
debt by hand at any time to free collateral sooner.

### Earmarking, and what it pays you

Redemptions land in cycles. When a cycle begins, the protocol earmarks a slice of
each open loan, in proportion to its share of total system debt. The earmarked
collateral stays in place until the claim settles, and it keeps earning yield until
then. The docs call this **temporal leverage**: the longer the gap between earmark
and settlement, the more yield the earmarked collateral collects.

Earmarked debt is repaid with MYT; the repay tab offers the right asset for each
part of a loan.
