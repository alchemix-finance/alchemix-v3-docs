- The Redemption Rate is a projection: the alUSD waiting on the Fixed Yield page, divided by the term in years, divided by the vault's Total Debt. 250,000 waiting on a 4-month term against 1,500,000 of Total Debt is 50%.
- Twice the alUSD waiting doubles the rate, while twice the Total Debt halves it, since the same payouts are shared across more debt.
- Earmarked debt is reserved as Fixed Yield deposits vest. The deposit behind it stays in your vault and keeps earning until the Fixed Yield depositor claims, which is when the redemption settles.
