### What Alchemix does

Deposit ETH or USDC and it earns from the day it lands. Borrow alUSD or alETH against
that deposit, up to 90% of its value. Hand the alUSD or alETH to the Transmuter and it
returns USDC or ETH 1:1 once the wait is up.

### The loan charges no interest

Nothing is added to the balance and nothing falls due on a schedule. The protocol
repays the loan out of your position while the deposit underneath keeps earning. Left
alone, the balance only moves down.

### How fast the balance falls

The pace depends on protocol conditions that change, so no date can be promised. The
green line you watched runs at an illustrative pace. Your own position shows live
figures in the app.
