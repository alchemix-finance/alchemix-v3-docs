### What Alchemix does

Deposit ETH or USDC and it earns from the day it lands. Borrow alUSD or alETH
against that deposit, up to 90% of its value. Hand the alUSD or alETH to the
Transmuter and it returns USDC or ETH at 1:1 once the wait is up.

### Your deposit pays the loan down

On most lending platforms you pay interest for as long as the loan is open, and the
collateral you posted sits there earning nothing. Alchemix runs it the other way
around. Your deposit keeps working the entire time it backs the loan, and what it
earns is what clears the balance.

### How fast the balance falls

That comes down to two things: what the strategies behind your deposit earn, and
how quickly redemptions run. Both move, so nobody can hand you a payoff date. The
green line you watched uses an example rate, and your own position shows live
figures in the app.
