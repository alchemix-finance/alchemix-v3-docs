### What Alchemix does

Deposit ETH or USDC and it starts earning. Borrow alUSD or alETH against that
deposit, up to 90% of its value, and sell it for spendable cash. Repay whenever
you want, or leave it alone and let redemptions clear the balance for you.

### Where the repayments come from

Redemptions repay your debt out of your own collateral, a slice at a time, while
the rest of that collateral stays in the vault and keeps earning. Each one
deleverages you: the debt and the collateral behind it shrink together. Your debt
and your collateral are the same asset, ETH behind alETH and USDC behind alUSD, so
being paid down this way costs you only a small redemption fee.

### The pace of repayment

Every loan in the market clears at the same redemption rate, and the rate moves
with how much alUSD savers have waiting to be redeemed. The figures in this lesson
run at an example rate. Your own vault prints the live one.
