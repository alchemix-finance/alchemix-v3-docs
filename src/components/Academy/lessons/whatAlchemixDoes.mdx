### Three things

Deposit ETH or USDC and earn on it. Borrow alUSD or alETH against the deposit, up to
90% of its value. Put alUSD or alETH into the Transmuter and receive USDC or ETH 1:1
after a wait.

### The loan

No interest is added and there is no payment schedule. The protocol repays the
balance out of the position, and the deposit keeps earning while it does. Left
alone, the balance only moves down.

### The pace

How fast a balance clears depends on protocol conditions that change, so no date can
be promised. The chart in this lesson uses an illustrative pace. The app shows the
live figures for your position.
