### What Alchemix does

Deposit ETH or USDC and it starts earning right away. Borrow alUSD or alETH
against that deposit, up to 90% of its value. Repay whenever you want, or leave
it alone and let redemptions clear the balance for you.

### Where the repayments come from

On most lending platforms you pay interest for as long as the loan is open, and
the collateral you posted sits there earning nothing. Alchemix runs it the other
way around. Redemptions repay your debt on a schedule, drawing on your own
collateral, while that collateral stays in the vault and keeps earning.

Your debt and your collateral are the same asset, so being repaid this way costs
you nothing beyond a small redemption fee.

### How fast the balance falls

The protocol's redemption rate decides that, and it moves with how much sits in
the Transmuter against how much debt the system carries. The green line you
watched runs at an example rate. Your own position shows live figures in the app.
