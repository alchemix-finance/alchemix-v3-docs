### What Alchemix does

Deposit ETH or USDC and it starts earning right away. Borrow alUSD or alETH
against that deposit, up to 90% of its value. Repay whenever you want, or leave
it alone and let redemptions clear the balance for you.

### Where the repayments come from

On most lending platforms you pay interest for as long as the loan is open, and
the collateral you posted earns nothing the whole time. Alchemix runs it the
other way around. Redemptions repay your debt on a schedule, out of your own
collateral, while that collateral stays in the vault and keeps earning.

That deleverages your position rather than charging you for it. Your debt and
your collateral are the same asset, so the only cost is a small redemption fee.

### How fast the balance falls

Alchemix sets a redemption rate, and that rate determines the speed. It moves with
how much alUSD is waiting in the Transmuter against how much debt is in the
system. The figures in this lesson use an example rate, and your own position
shows live ones in the app.
