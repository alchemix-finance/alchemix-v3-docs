<Beside />

You now take the other side of a loan like yours, as the buyer of the alUSD a borrower sells. On <Term id="fixedYield">Fixed Yield</Term>, you earn a fixed return on that alUSD: before you deposit, you know the cost, the return and the day you can claim.

You deposit <Term id="alusd">alUSD</Term> for a set term and receive one USDC for each alUSD when the term ends. alUSD typically trades for slightly less than a dollar, so you pay less for each alUSD than the one USDC you get back for it.

The USDC you receive comes from borrowers in an Alchemix vault, out of their repayments and their deposits. Once you deposit, your payout is scaled down only if that vault carries bad debt, a loss its borrowers' deposits cannot cover.

<Do moment="claim" title="Claim at maturity">

<ClaimMoment />

You received 5,000 USDC in your <Stat name="wallet">practice wallet</Stat>, one for each <Term id="alusd">alUSD</Term> you deposited. Your 5,000 alUSD was burned, which means destroyed, because the same amount of borrowers' debt was canceled.

The USDC came from the <Term id="transmuter">Transmuter</Term>. The Transmuter pays you first out of any <Term id="myt">MYT</Term> it already holds from borrowers' repayments and from <Term id="liquidation">liquidations</Term>, and takes the rest from borrowers' deposits through a <Term id="redemption">redemption</Term>.

The Transmuter pays in MYT. In the app's claim window, the "Receive USDC upon claim" switch is on by default, and while it is on, the app exchanges the MYT for the USDC it is worth.

<PageBreak title="Where your payout comes from" />

<Beside />

The USDC you claimed was paid by a borrower. Following the 5,000 <Term id="alusd">alUSD</Term> from the borrower's loan to your claim shows where your 150 return comes from.

In this example, the <Term id="transmuter">Transmuter</Term> holds no <Term id="myt">MYT</Term> from repayments or <Term id="liquidation">liquidations</Term>, so all 5,000 comes from the borrower's deposit, held as MYT in the borrower's vault. Press Next step at the bottom of the page to follow the alUSD.

<LoopSequence>

1. The borrower borrows 5,000 alUSD from the borrower's vault and swaps it for 4,850 USDC. Selling below a dollar costs the borrower 150, the <Term id="discount">discount</Term>.
2. You buy that 5,000 alUSD for 4,850 USDC and deposit it on <Term id="fixedYield">Fixed Yield</Term>. The Transmuter holds your alUSD while your deposit vests.
3. As your deposit vests, the borrower's vault earmarks 5,000 of the borrower's debt to pay you. The borrower's deposit keeps earning in the meantime.
4. When you claim, the Transmuter takes 5,000 in MYT from the borrower's vault, cancels 5,000 of the borrower's debt, pays you 5,000 USDC and burns your alUSD. The borrower's vault also takes a 12.50 <Term id="redemptionFee">redemption fee</Term> (0.25% of the 5,000) from the borrower's deposit for the protocol.

</LoopSequence>

Your 150 return is the 150 discount the borrower paid when they sold their alUSD.

<Beside />

You can also claim before the maturity date. Your deposit vests evenly over the term, so an early claim pays the vested share as USDC and returns the rest as <Term id="alusd">alUSD</Term>, minus the <Term id="earlyExitFee">early exit fee</Term>.

Say you needed the money halfway through the term. <There />, the practice Dashboard is set on <Stat name="date">Jan 03, 2027</Stat>, with your 5,000 alUSD still deposited.

<Do moment="claim-early" title="Claim halfway through">

Press <Stat name="claim">Claim with Penalty</Stat>.

</Do>

<ClaimEarlyMoment />

Your <Stat name="wallet">practice wallet</Stat> received 2,500 USDC and 2,475 alUSD. Half the term had passed, so half your deposit had vested and was paid as USDC.

The unvested 2,500 came back as alUSD, minus a 25 early exit fee: 1.00% of the unvested part, on Ethereum at the time of writing. The fee discourages depositors from leaving before the term ends.

You can swap that alUSD on the Trade page, or deposit it on a new card.

<ReadOn moment="read-early-paid" title="What an early claim pays" />

Claiming halfway left you with a gain of about 51, against 150 if you had waited for the maturity date.

The vested half still earned its full return. The unvested half came back as alUSD in place of USDC, and alUSD typically trades for slightly less than a dollar, so that half gave up its return and paid the 25 early exit fee too.
