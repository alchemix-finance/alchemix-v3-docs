An Alchemix loan has no interest rate, so the balance moves in one direction, down,
unless you choose to mint more. The cost is real. It just shows up somewhere else:
once when you sell the alAsset, and again when redemptions repay your debt. Both
charges are tied to events rather than to the calendar.

### The market discount

alAssets are minted at face value, and they can trade below it. Selling borrowed
alUSD for spendable USDC realizes that gap immediately, while the debt recorded
inside the vault stays at face value.

- **Borrowing 5,000 alUSD at a price of 0.96** puts about 4,800 USDC in your hand,
  against 5,000 of recorded debt.
- **To receive 5,000 in hand**, you have to borrow about 5,208 instead.

The cost scales with the size of the borrow. You pay it once, at the moment you
sell, and holding the position never adds to it.

### The borrower redemption fee

When redemptions repay part of your debt using your collateral, a small fraction of
the amount repaid goes to the protocol treasury. The same fee applies whenever
collateral settles earmarked debt, a self-liquidation included.

The fee is charged per event rather than per year, so its effective annual cost depends
on your starting LTV and on how long a transmutation takes. It works out roughly
as:

> Fee × (1 year ÷ transmutation time) × starting LTV

Current rates for this and every other fee are read live from the contracts on the
[Fees](/user/concepts/fees) page, since governance can change them.

The discount that costs you is a return for whoever buys your alUSD below 1.00. The
peg and the discount lesson puts a number on that return.
