- The discount is a one-time cost, paid on the day you swap your alUSD. It is Debt × (1 − alUSD price), which is 100 on 5,000 at 0.98.
- A redemption takes the same amount off your Deposit and your Debt, so your Net Value moves only by the redemption fee, 0.25% of what the redemption pays off.
- At a 50% Redemption Rate, a year of both costs came to about 105, while the same 5,000 borrowed elsewhere at 7% interest cost 350.
