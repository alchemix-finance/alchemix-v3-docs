An Alchemix loan charges no interest. The balance moves in one direction, down,
unless you choose to mint more. What you pay arrives in two places. Both are tied
to events, and no clock runs on either.

### The market discount

alAssets are minted at face value, and they can trade below it. Selling borrowed
alUSD for spendable USDC realizes that gap immediately, while the debt recorded
inside the vault stays at face value.

- **Borrowing 5,000 alUSD at a price of 0.97** puts about 4,850 USDC in your hand,
  against 5,000 of recorded debt.
- **To receive 5,000 in hand**, you have to borrow about 5,155 instead.

The cost scales with the size of the borrow, and holding it longer adds nothing.

### The borrower redemption fee

When redemptions repay part of your debt using your collateral, a small fraction of
the amount repaid goes to the protocol treasury. The same fee applies whenever
collateral is used to settle earmarked debt, including during a self-liquidation.

Because the fee is charged per event, its effective annual cost
depends on your starting LTV and how long a transmutation takes, roughly:

> Fee × (1 year ÷ transmutation time) × starting LTV

Current rates for this and every other fee are read live from the contracts on the
[Fees](/user/concepts/fees) page, since governance can change them.

The market discount is also an opportunity for whoever buys alUSD below 1.00. The
peg and the discount lesson works out that return.
