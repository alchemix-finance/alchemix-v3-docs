Your deposit is held as the Mix-Yield Token, a vault built on Morpho Vaults V2 that
routes deposits into several yield sources at once. You hold one token. The DAO
selects the strategies behind it, sets target weights, and rebalances as markets
shift.

Every strategy carries a classification, and the classification sets a ceiling on
how much of the vault it may occupy.

- **Conservative** – No cap applies. The class is reserved for strategies that wrap
  and unwrap on contract, price off their own backing, and carry no duration risk.
- **Moderate** – Any single strategy is capped at 25%, and the class is capped at
  40% in total.
- **Aggressive** – Any single strategy is capped at 10%, and the class is capped at
  10% in total.

### Why the ceilings matter to a borrower

The caps exist so that users can set an LTV that keeps liquidation risk low.

A price move cannot close an Alchemix position, because debt and collateral are
like-kind. Only a loss inside the vault itself can push a position toward
liquidation. A composition allowed to hold far more risk would make a high LTV
considerably more dangerous, so the ceilings on the vault protect your borrowing
headroom.

A cut is taken before the yield reaches you. A performance fee, a share of what the
strategies earn, goes to the protocol before the remainder is credited to the MYT
price. The current rate is read live on the [Fees](/user/concepts/fees) page.

The Choosing an LTV lesson works out what these same caps mean for the number you
pick.
