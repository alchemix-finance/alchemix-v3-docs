- The Alchemix DAO picks the strategies in each vault, sets each one's share of the vault and gives it a Risk Level: Conservative, Moderate or Aggressive. The vault keeps a 15% performance fee from what they earn before the rest reaches the MYT price.
- Caps limit the riskier levels, allowing at most 20% of a vault in Aggressive strategies and 60% in Moderate and Aggressive together, so no more than 60% of a vault can sit outside Conservative.
- Every deposit in a vault holds the same mix of strategies, so a loss in one strategy lowers every deposit by that strategy's share times its loss.
