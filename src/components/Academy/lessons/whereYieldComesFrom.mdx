Your deposit is held as the Mix-Yield Token, a vault built on Morpho Vaults V2 that
routes deposits into several yield sources at once. You hold one token. The DAO
selects the strategies behind it, sets target weights, and rebalances as markets
shift.

Every strategy carries a classification, and the classification sets a ceiling:

- **Conservative** – No cap. Reserved for strategies with contract wrap and unwrap,
  fundamental pricing, and no duration risk.
- **Moderate** – 25% for any single strategy, 40% across all of them.
- **Aggressive** – 10% for any single strategy, and 10% across all of them.

### Why the ceilings matter to a borrower

The caps exist so that users can set an LTV that keeps liquidation risk low.

A price move cannot close an Alchemix position, because debt and collateral are
like-kind. Only a loss inside the vault itself can push a position toward
liquidation. A composition allowed to hold far more risk would make a high LTV
considerably more dangerous, so the ceilings on the vault protect your borrowing
headroom.

One deduction happens before yield reaches you. A performance fee, a share of what
the strategies earn, goes to the protocol before the remainder is credited to the
MYT price. The current rate is read live on the [Fees](/user/concepts/fees) page.

The Choosing an LTV lesson works out what the same caps mean for the LTV you choose.
