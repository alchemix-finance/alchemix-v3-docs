### Borrowing stops at 90%

You can borrow up to 90% of what you deposited. That ratio is your **loan to
value**, or LTV: what you owe divided by what you deposited. Reach 90% and the
protocol stops you borrowing more. Your position stays open, keeps earning,
and sitting at the cap costs you nothing. The Borrow tab’s Max button fills in the
number for you.

### alUSD lands in your wallet

Borrowing mints alUSD against USDC, or alETH against ETH, and sends it straight to
you. Inside the protocol one alUSD always cancels one USDC of debt. Out on the open
market it trades a little below 1.00, and the cost lesson puts a number on that gap.

### Your deposit stays put

Every unit of it stays in the vault and keeps earning while the loan is open. It is
now also the **collateral** standing behind your debt.
