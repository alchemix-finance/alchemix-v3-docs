### Borrowing stops at 90%

You can borrow up to 90% of what you deposited. That ratio is your **loan to
value**, or LTV: what you owe divided by what you deposited. Reach 90% and Alchemix
stops you borrowing more. Your position stays open, keeps earning,
and sitting at the cap costs you nothing. The Borrow tab’s Max button fills in the
number for you.

The app also prints a **health factor** next to your LTV. It is the same distance
to the cap, written as a multiple: 3.00 at 30% LTV, 1.00 once you reach the cap,
and unbounded while you have no debt at all.

### alUSD lands in your wallet

Borrowing mints alUSD against USDC, or alETH against ETH, and sends it straight to
you. Inside the protocol one alUSD always cancels one USDC of debt. On the open market it
trades a little below 1.00, and the cost lesson puts a number on that gap.

### Your deposit stays put

Every unit of it stays in the vault and keeps earning while the loan is open. It is
now also the **collateral** securing your debt.

### What people borrow for

Borrowers put the alAsset to work in a few standard ways:

- **Spending it.** Large purchases, or a periodic draw against a position that
  keeps earning, without selling the position.
- **Yield looping.** Swap the borrowed alAsset for the underlying, deposit that
  too, and take amplified exposure to the vault's yield. The vault page has a tab
  for it.
- **Liquidity provision.** Pair the alAsset against its underlying, where the
  impermanent loss is bounded by how far the peg moves.
- **Short-term opportunities.** Move capital now and keep the position intact.

Each of these starts by selling the alAsset, and that sale is where an Alchemix
loan actually costs you. The intermediate track prices it.
