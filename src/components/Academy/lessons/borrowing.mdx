### Borrowing stops at 90%

You can borrow up to 90% of what you deposited. That ratio is your **loan to
value**, or LTV: what you owe divided by what you deposited. Reaching 90% stops
further borrowing. Nothing is sold and nothing is charged when you get there. The
Borrow tab’s Max button fills in the cap for you.

### alUSD lands in your wallet

Borrowing creates alUSD against USDC, or alETH against ETH, and sends it to you.
Inside the protocol one alUSD counts as one USDC. On the open market it may trade a
little below 1.00.

### The deposit stays where it is

Borrowing takes nothing out of the vault. The deposit becomes the **collateral**
behind the loan, and all of it stays in and keeps earning while the loan is open.
