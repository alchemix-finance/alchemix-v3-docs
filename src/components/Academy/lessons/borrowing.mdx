### The cap

You can borrow up to 90% of what you deposited. That ratio is your **loan to
value**, or LTV: what you owe divided by what you deposited. Reaching 90% stops
further borrowing. Nothing is sold and nothing is charged. The Borrow tab’s Max
button fills in the cap for you.

### What arrives

Borrowing creates alUSD against USDC or alETH against ETH in your wallet. Inside
the protocol one alUSD counts as one USDC. On the open market it may trade a
little below 1.00.

### What stays

Borrowing takes nothing out of the vault. The deposit becomes the **collateral**
for the loan, and all of it stays in and keeps earning while the loan is open.
