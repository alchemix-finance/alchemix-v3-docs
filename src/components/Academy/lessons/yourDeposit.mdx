### What the deposit becomes

The vault wraps your USDC or ETH into the **Mix-Yield Token**, or MYT, a share of a
whole basket of yield strategies at once. The Alchemix DAO picks those strategies
and rebalances them as markets move, so the work of hunting down a better rate is
already handled.

### How it earns

Each MYT is worth a little more of the underlying asset every day, as the
strategies behind it earn. That is where your yield shows up: in the value of what
you already hold, credited continuously. The Dashboard shows what your position is
worth today.

### Leaving whenever you want

Withdraw any amount on any day, and the yield earned so far comes out with it.
Borrowing against the deposit is a separate decision, and plenty of people skip it
and just hold the MYT.
