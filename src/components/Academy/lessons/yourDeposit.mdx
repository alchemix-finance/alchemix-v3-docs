### What the deposit becomes

The vault wraps your USDC or ETH into the **Mix-Yield Token**, or MYT, a share of a
basket of yield strategies. The Alchemix DAO picks those strategies and rebalances
them as markets move.

### How it earns

Each MYT is worth a little more of the underlying asset every day, as the
strategies behind it earn. That is where your yield shows up, in the value of
what you already hold. The Dashboard shows what your position is worth today.

### Which screen to use

The Borrow page holds the vaults you can borrow against, and its Deposit/Borrow tab
takes the deposit and the loan in one transaction. The Mixed Yield page, under
Earn, lists the same vaults for someone who wants the yield on its own. This track
borrows, so it stays on Borrow.

Every vault has a **deposit cap**, drawn on its card as a bar with how full it is.
A vault sitting at its cap accepts nothing more until the DAO raises it, so it is
worth a look before you plan a deposit around one.

### Leaving whenever you want

Withdraw any amount on any day, and the yield earned so far is returned with it.
Borrowing against the deposit is optional.
