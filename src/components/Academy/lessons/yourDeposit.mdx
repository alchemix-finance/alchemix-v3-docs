### What the deposit becomes

The vault wraps your USDC or ETH into the **Mix-Yield Token**, or MYT, a share of a
set of yield strategies. The Alchemix DAO chooses the strategies and rebalances
them, so there is nothing for you to manage.

### How it earns

There is no payout to claim. Each MYT becomes worth more of the underlying asset as
the strategies earn, and the Dashboard shows the current value of your position.

### Getting out

There is no lock-up and no notice period. Withdraw at any time, in any amount, and
the yield earned so far comes with it. Borrowing against the deposit is a separate
decision.
