### What the deposit becomes

The vault wraps your USDC or ETH into the **Mix-Yield Token**, or MYT, a share of a
set of yield strategies. The Alchemix DAO chooses those strategies and rebalances
them as conditions change, so there is nothing for you to manage.

### How it earns

No payout ever arrives for you to claim. Each MYT becomes worth more of the
underlying asset as the strategies earn, and the Dashboard shows what your position is
worth today.

### Nothing locks it in

There is no lock-up and no notice period. Withdraw at any time, in any amount, and the
yield earned so far comes out with it. Borrowing against the deposit is a separate
decision.
