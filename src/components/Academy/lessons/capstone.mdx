A deposit big enough to raise the capital you need can still be too small to
survive the loss you expect. Both have to hold at once, and the two numbers constrain
each other.

**How much you must borrow** is set by the price you can sell at. Raising a given
amount of spendable capital means borrowing that amount divided by the market
price.

**How much collateral that borrow needs behind it** is set by the loss you have to
be able to absorb. The highest starting LTV that survives a loss is 95% of whatever
backing remains.

Put together, the smallest deposit that does both is:

> Deposit = (capital ÷ price) ÷ (95% × (1 − loss))

A worse discount means borrowing more to raise the same capital. The larger borrow lifts your LTV, so that same loss now needs more
collateral behind it. You have to solve for both at once.

### The tool that does this for you

The app runs this arithmetic for you. Every vault has a **Visualizer** tab taking
four inputs you now know:

- **Yield APY** – what the strategies earn, from the Mix-Yield Token lesson.
- **Redemption** – the pace your debt clears at, from the pace lesson.
- **alAsset price** – the discount, from the cost and peg lessons.
- **External APY** – what you earn on the capital you raised, which is the one
  input this track never gave you, because it depends on what you do with the money.

It projects the deposit, the debt and the net value across a horizon you choose,
and reports the loan cost, the aggregate yield, and the profit left over. Put your
own figures into it before you commit to a position.
