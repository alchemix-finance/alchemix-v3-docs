### The exchange

The Transmuter takes alUSD or alETH and returns the underlying asset 1:1 after a
term: one alUSD for one USDC, one alETH for one ETH. The market price of the
alAsset makes no difference to what you receive. Payment arrives as MYT, which
the app turns back into USDC or ETH.

### The term

The Alchemix DAO sets the term, and it differs by asset and chain. The app shows
the current term before you deposit. Waiting the full term costs nothing. Leaving
early carries a fee, and the app shows it in advance.

### Why the price stays near 1.00

Borrowers often sell the alUSD they just borrowed, which can push the market
price a little under 1.00. When it trades below 1.00, buying it and waiting pays
the gap, and that buying pulls the price back toward 1.00. The intermediate track
prices the gap.
