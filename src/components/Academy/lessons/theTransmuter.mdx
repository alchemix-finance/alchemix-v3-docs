### What the Transmuter is for

The Transmuter pays a fixed rate. You buy alUSD or alETH below face value,
deposit it, wait out a governance-set term, and receive the underlying at exactly
1:1. One alUSD returns one USDC and one alETH returns one ETH, whatever the
alAsset was trading at when you bought it. Payment arrives as MYT, which the app
unwraps into USDC or ETH for you.

On the Fixed Yield page this is a single action. **Swap & Deposit** takes the asset
you already hold, buys the alAsset at the market price, and deposits it into the
queue in one transaction.

### Reading the card

Each position on that page states the same six things:

- **Projected fixed APR.** The discount you are buying at, annualized over the term.
- **Maturity date.** The day the 1:1 exchange is available to you.
- **Term.** How long the wait is, which differs by asset and by chain.
- **Early exit fee.** What leaving before maturity costs. The part that has
  matured is paid out, and the rest comes back as alUSD less the fee.
- **Deposit cap.** How much more the queue will take.
- **alAsset price.** What the market is charging for the alAsset right now.

The DAO sets the term and the fees, and the price moves, so read the card before you deposit.

### Why the price stays near 1.00

Borrowers often sell the alUSD they just borrowed, and that selling pushes the
market price a little under 1.00. Every time it dips, buying it and waiting
out the term pays more, so buyers step in and push the price back up.

### What this has to do with your loan

The queue is what funds redemptions. As each alAsset in the Transmuter matures,
it earmarks an equal value of borrower collateral, and when the saver claims,
that collateral settles the claim and repays the borrower's debt. The saver's
fixed rate and the borrower's repayment are the same event, seen from two sides.
That is where the Earmarked figure on your own position came from in lesson 4.
