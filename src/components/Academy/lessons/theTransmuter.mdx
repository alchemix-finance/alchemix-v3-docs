### What you get back

Hand the Transmuter your alUSD or alETH, wait out a term, and it returns the
underlying asset at exactly 1:1. One alUSD comes back as one USDC, one alETH as one
ETH. Whatever the alAsset was trading at when you deposited it, you still collect
the full unit. Payment arrives as MYT, which the app turns back into USDC or ETH
for you.

### How long you wait

The Alchemix DAO sets the term, and it differs by asset and by chain, so the app
shows you the current one before you deposit. Wait it out and you keep the whole
1:1. Leave early and you pay a fee, quoted to you up front.

### Why the price stays near 1.00

Borrowers often sell the alUSD they just borrowed, and that selling pushes the
market price a little under 1.00. Every time it dips, buying the discount and
waiting out the term pays the difference, so buyers step in and lift it back. The
intermediate track prices that trade.
