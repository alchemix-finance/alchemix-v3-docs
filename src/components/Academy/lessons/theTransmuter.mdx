### What the Transmuter is for

The Transmuter pays a fixed rate. You buy alUSD or alETH below face value,
deposit it, wait out a governance-set term, and receive the underlying at exactly
1:1. One alUSD returns one USDC and one alETH returns one ETH, whatever the
alAsset was trading at when you bought it. Payment arrives as MYT, which the app
unwraps into USDC or ETH for you.

### How long you wait

The Alchemix DAO sets the term, and it differs by asset and by chain, so the app
shows you the current one before you deposit. Wait it out and you receive the full
1:1. Leave early and you pay a fee, quoted to you up front.

### Why the price stays near 1.00

Borrowers often sell the alUSD they just borrowed, and that selling pushes the
market price a little under 1.00. Every time it dips, buying it and waiting
out the term pays more, so buyers step in and push the price back up.

### What this has to do with your loan

The queue is what funds redemptions. Every alAsset waiting in the Transmuter
earmarks an equal value of borrower collateral, and at maturity that collateral
settles the claim and repays the borrower's debt. The saver's fixed rate and the
borrower's repayment are the same event, seen from two sides.

If you are the borrower and you want your collateral back, repay and withdraw.
Inside Alchemix one alUSD always cancels one USDC of debt, so the alUSD you
borrowed is what you repay with.
