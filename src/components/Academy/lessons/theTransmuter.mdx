### What you get back

The Transmuter takes your alUSD or alETH and returns the underlying asset at 1:1
once a term is up. One alUSD comes back as one USDC, and one alETH as one ETH. The
market price of the alAsset makes no difference to what you receive. Payment
arrives as MYT, which the app turns back into USDC or ETH.

### How long you wait

The Alchemix DAO sets the term, and it differs by asset and by chain. The app shows
you the current one before you deposit. Waiting it out costs nothing, while leaving
early carries a fee that the app shows you in advance.

### Why the price stays near 1.00

Borrowers often sell the alUSD they just borrowed, which can push the market price
a little under 1.00. Whenever it trades below 1.00, buying it and waiting out the
term pays the difference, and that buying pulls the price back up. The intermediate
track prices the gap.
