### What a price move does

Your debt and your deposit are the same kind of asset: alETH against ETH, alUSD
against USDC. When the price moves, both sides move together and the ratio between
them stays put. A price move cannot liquidate an Alchemix position.

### What can

A loss inside the Mix-Yield Token, from a hack of a strategy or a strategy losing
money. The deposit is worth less while the debt is unchanged, and the liquidation
marker slides toward the position. Past it, only the minimum needed is sold to cover
the debt. The rest is untouched.

### Where to sit

The lower your LTV, the larger a loss the position absorbs. The 90% cap only stops
borrowing. Liquidation starts at the 95% marker. The intermediate track works out the
number.
