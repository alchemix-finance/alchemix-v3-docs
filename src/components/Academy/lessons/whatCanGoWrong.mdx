### What a price move does

Your debt and your deposit are the same kind of asset. ETH backs alETH, and USDC
backs alUSD. When the price moves, both sides move together and the ratio between
them holds. A price move on its own cannot liquidate an Alchemix position.

### What can liquidate

A real loss inside the Mix-Yield Token can. A strategy is exploited, or it simply
loses money, and your deposit is suddenly worth less while the debt stands where it
was. The liquidation marker slides toward your position. Past it, only the minimum
needed to cover the debt is sold, and the rest keeps earning.

### Where to set your LTV

The lower your LTV, the larger a loss of backing your position absorbs before any of
this reaches you. Hitting the 90% cap only stops you borrowing more, and liquidation
does not begin until the 95% marker. The intermediate track works out the number.
