### What a price move does

On most lending platforms, liquidation is a price problem. The market falls far
enough, and the protocol sells your collateral to cover the loan. Alchemix takes
price out of that equation. Your debt and your deposit are the same kind of asset,
ETH behind alETH and USDC behind alUSD, so the two sides move together and the
ratio between them holds. A 40% drawdown leaves your position exactly where it was.

### What can liquidate

One thing does: a real loss inside the Mix-Yield Token. A strategy gets exploited,
or it simply loses money, and your deposit is suddenly worth less while your debt is
unchanged. The liquidation marker slides toward you. Cross it and the
protocol sells just enough to bring the position back to a safe ratio. The rest
stays yours and keeps earning.

### Where to set your LTV

The lower you borrow, the larger a loss your position can absorb before any of this
reaches you. The 90% cap only stops you borrowing more. Liquidation begins at 95%.

The vault's own risk caps turn that into a number. The DAO can never put more than
20% of an MYT into its riskiest class of strategy, so suppose that entire slice went
to zero: 95% of the 80% still standing is 76%, and a position opened at or below 76%
survives it. Open lower and you have more room than that.

The intermediate track turns this into a rule for any loss you want to size
against, and shows where the caps come from.
