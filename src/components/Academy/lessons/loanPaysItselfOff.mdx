<Beside />

You aren't required to take any further action on your position. From here the loan gets paid off automatically through Alchemix v3's redemption system.

<There /> is the practice vault with your position: a <Stat name="deposit">Deposit</Stat> of 10,000 USDC and a <Stat name="debt">Debt</Stat> of 5,000 <Term id="alusd">alUSD</Term>.

A <Term id="redemption">redemption</Term> removes <Term id="myt">MYT</Term> from your deposit and cancels the same amount of your debt, effectively deleveraging your position.

Your deposit minus your debt is what you would have left if you closed the loan today. A redemption lowers both by the same amount, so what you would have left stays the same, apart from a small fee.

<ReadOn moment="read-redemption" title="What a redemption does" />

Redemptions exist to pay people who deposit alUSD on the Fixed Yield page. Each depositor is owed one USDC for each alUSD, and the MYT taken from borrowers' deposits pays them when they claim.

Any MYT that borrowers have already repaid pays the depositor first, and redemptions pay only the rest.

You received your loan's value up front, as the 5,000 alUSD you borrowed and swapped. Redemptions then settle the loan from your deposit over time, so you never have to bring new money to pay it back.

The deposit that is left keeps earning.

<ReadOn moment="read-paid" title="Who a redemption pays" />

The <Stat name="redemption">Redemption Rate</Stat> on the practice vault shows how fast redemptions are paying loans down, as a share of the debt each year.

Each deposit on the Fixed Yield page vests a little at a time over its term, until its maturity date, when the depositor can claim it. The alUSD that vests is paid for out of borrowers' deposits, through redemptions.

The rate rises when more alUSD is vesting, because more debt has to be redeemed to pay it. The rate falls when borrowers owe more in total, because the same payout is a smaller share of their debt.

<Work title="How the Redemption Rate is worked out">

The rate takes the alUSD set to vest over the next week, scales it up to a year and divides it by everything borrowers in the vault owe.

</Work>

Each vault has its own borrowers and its own Fixed Yield deposits, so the rate differs from vault to vault and chain to chain.

<ReadOn moment="read-rate" title="What sets the Redemption Rate" />

In the app, a redemption can happen each time a Fixed Yield depositor claims, so a loan is paid down in many small steps through the year. To keep the figures easy to follow, the practice vault runs one redemption a month.

For the sake of example, the practice vault's <Stat name="redemption">Redemption Rate</Stat> reads 50%. At that rate, redemptions would pay 2,500 of your 5,000 debt in a year, about 208 a month.

So the first month's redemption is 208. Each redemption after that is worked out on the debt that is left, so each one is a little smaller than the last.

<ReadOn moment="read-redeemed" title="What the redemption changed" />

Your debt and your deposit fell by the same 208, so the redemption cost you only the <Term id="redemptionFee">redemption fee</Term>. On this 208 the fee was 0.52, taken from your deposit and paid to the protocol.

The redemption fee is 0.25% of each amount a redemption pays off on Ethereum, where this lesson's vault is. Other chains set their own, listed on the [Fees page](/user/concepts/fees) in the Alchemix docs.

Your <Stat name="ltv">LTV</Stat> fell from 50% to 48.9%. The same 208 came off both sides, and 208 is a bigger share of your 5,000 debt than of your 10,000 deposit.

A lower LTV leaves more room under the 90% limit, so <Stat name="borrowable">Borrowable</Stat> rose by 20.33.

<ReadOn moment="read-fee" title="The fee and your LTV" />

The redemption's 208 paid a Fixed Yield depositor. The <Term id="myt">MYT</Term> taken from your deposit went to the <Term id="transmuter">Transmuter</Term>, the Alchemix contract that holds Fixed Yield deposits while they vest and pays depositors when they claim.

Earmarked debt is still your debt. The part of your deposit set aside to pay it keeps earning yield until the Fixed Yield depositor claims, which is when the redemption happens.

Every borrower in the vault shares the earmarking. Each one has the same share of their debt set aside, counted on the part not yet earmarked, so a borrower who owes more has more earmarked.

<ReadOn moment="read-year-deposit" title="Your loan and deposit after a year" />

Most lenders charge interest, so a loan grows while you owe it. The dashed <Stat name="line-lender">Lender loan</Stat> is the same 5,000 borrowed at 7% a year, to show what the year would cost with a lender instead.

With the lender, your whole 10,000 stays in the vault and earns 5% all year. With Alchemix, redemptions pay your debt down out of your deposit, which earns the same 5%.
