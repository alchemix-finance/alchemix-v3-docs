- alUSD typically trades under the peg because borrowers sell it for USDC. It comes back toward a dollar because inside Alchemix it always repays a full dollar, which gives borrowers and Fixed Yield depositors a reason to buy it.
- A Fixed Yield card's return for its term is (1 − price) ÷ price: about 2.04% at 0.98, or about 6.1% a year on a 4-month term.
- alUSD and Flash Repay repay only debt that is not earmarked, each alUSD repaying a full dollar of it, so 1,000 alUSD bought for 980 USDC pays off 1,000 of Debt.
