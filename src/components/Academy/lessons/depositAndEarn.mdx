<Beside />

To earn yield on USDC and be able to borrow against it later, you deposit it into a vault. A vault is one pool of USDC or ETH that Alchemix spreads across yield <Term id="strategy">strategies</Term> on other protocols.

<There /> are two vault cards from the app's Borrow page, which has one card for each vault. You pick a vault for the asset you hold and the chain you want.

<ReadOn moment="read-vault" title="What a vault is" />

<Shot name="borrowCards">Two vault cards on the Borrow page.</Shot>

Each card on the Borrow page shows what you need to pick a vault.

</Change>
<Change stat="card-chain">

This lesson uses the <Stat name="card-name">Risk-adjusted Mix USDC</Stat> vault on Ethereum as its example.

<PageBreak title="Where your USDC goes" />

<Beside />

<There /> is the <Stat name="info-tab">Info</Stat> tab from a vault's page in the app. The <Stat name="info-tab">Info</Stat> tab shows where the vault puts your USDC.

Your USDC goes into several <Term id="strategy">strategies</Term>: lending markets, liquid staking and other vaults. Each one is listed under <Stat name="info-strategy">Strategy</Stat>, and its <Stat name="info-alloc">Allocation</Stat> is the share of the vault that strategy holds.

The Alchemix DAO chooses the strategies and sets each one's share. Spreading deposits across several of them reduces the vault's exposure to any single protocol.

The mix differs from vault to vault and chain to chain.

<ReadOn moment="read-strategies" title="The vault's strategies" />

<Shot name="vaultInfo" column>A vault's Info tab, with the vault's APR over the last 30 days.</Shot>

Each strategy on the <Stat name="info-tab">Info</Stat> tab has a <Stat name="info-risk">Risk Level</Stat>: the Alchemix DAO's rating of how much could go wrong with the protocol behind the strategy. The app prints each rating in words, such as Conservative or Moderate.

The riskier a Risk Level, the smaller the cap on how much of the vault's deposits its strategies can hold. Those caps keep more of your deposit in the strategies rated less risky.

<ReadOn moment="read-risk" title="Risk Level" />

A vault's <Term id="apr">APR</Term> is how much a deposit would grow in a year, as a percentage, if the vault's strategies kept earning at today's rates. Each strategy's own rate is listed under <Stat name="info-apr">APR</Stat> on the <Stat name="info-tab">Info</Stat> tab.

The APR moves every day, as the <Stat name="info-chart">chart</Stat> of the last 30 days shows.

The same vaults are on the <Term id="mixedYield">Mixed Yield</Term> page, for depositing without a loan. A deposit made there goes into the same strategies as one made from the Borrow page, so it earns the same yield.

<PageBreak title="Make the deposit" />

<Beside />

<There /> is a practice copy of the Risk-adjusted Mix USDC vault's page, with nothing deposited yet. In the app, a vault's card on the Borrow page opens the vault's page, where you deposit and borrow in one flow.

The vault's <Stat name="deposit">Deposit</Stat> stat reads 0 and shows your deposit once you make it.

<Do moment="deposit" title="Deposit your 10,000 USDC">

Deposit the 10,000 USDC in your <Stat name="wallet">practice wallet</Stat>: type 10,000 in the <Stat name="field">USDC field</Stat> and press <Stat name="action">Deposit</Stat>.

</Do>

<DepositMoment />

The vault deposited your 10,000 USDC into <Term id="myt">MYT</Term>, the Mix-Yield Token, at MYT's current exchange rate. The vault holds your MYT as your position's collateral.

The <Stat name="deposit">Deposit</Stat> stat shows what your MYT is worth in USDC.

Your <Term id="myt">MYT</Term> is your share of the vault's <Term id="strategy">strategies</Term>. As the strategies earn, the exchange rate rises and each MYT is worth more USDC.

No new MYT is added to your position. If a strategy records a loss, each MYT is worth less.

<PageBreak title="A year of yield" />

<Beside />

The practice vault <There low /> holds your 10,000 USDC deposit. Its <Stat name="apr">APR</Stat> stat reads 5%. These lessons use 5% as an example rate, and a real vault's page shows its current APR.

Skipping ahead a year adds a year of yield at 5% to your <Stat name="deposit">Deposit</Stat>.

<Do moment="year" title="Skip ahead one year">

Press Skip ahead a year at the bottom of the page.

</Do>

<YearThenWithdrawMoment />

A year at 5% added 500 to your 10,000 deposit, so <Stat name="deposit">Deposit</Stat> reads 10,500. Your position holds the same <Term id="myt">MYT</Term> as before, now worth 500 more.

While you owe nothing against your deposit, you can withdraw any amount of it at any time. Withdrawing 500 takes the year's yield out as USDC.

<Do moment="withdraw" title="Withdraw the year's yield">

On the <Stat name="tab-withdraw">Withdraw</Stat> tab, type 500 in the <Stat name="field">USDC field</Stat>. Then press <Stat name="action">Withdraw</Stat>.

</Do>

<Await moment="withdraw" hint="Type 500 and press Withdraw" />

The vault sent the year's 500 of yield to your <Stat name="wallet">practice wallet</Stat> as USDC. <Stat name="deposit">Deposit</Stat> reads 10,000 again, the amount you started with.
