A position card shows you three numbers: what you deposited, what you owe, and how
much of the deposit you can take out today.

The amount you can take out moves two ways. Withdraw whatever is already free, which costs
you nothing. Or repay first, and every unit you repay frees more than a unit of
collateral, because each unit of debt requires about 1.11 units of collateral at
the cap. Clear the balance outright and the whole deposit unlocks.

### Where the withdrawable figure comes from

Take a position with 10,000 deposited and 5,000 borrowed. That debt needs about
5,556 of collateral behind it at the cap, which leaves about 4,444 free to
withdraw.

It is the same 90% cap that limits borrowing, read backwards. When you borrow, it
sets the most you can take out. When you withdraw, it sets the least you have to
leave in, and the app runs it both ways for you.

### Every stat on the screen

- **Health factor** – The distance to the borrowing cap, written as a multiple.
  A position at 30% LTV against a 90% cap reads 3.00, a position at the cap reads
  1.00, and a position with no debt is unbounded. It carries the same information as the bar.
- **Earmarked** – The protocol repays loans in cycles. While a cycle is under
  way, part of your loan shows as earmarked, meaning set aside for the next
  redemption. That part gets repaid with the Mix-Yield Token, or MYT, the token your
  deposit is held as, rather than with alUSD, and the asset menu in the repay tab offers
  you only what is valid. Earmarked collateral keeps earning until it settles.
- **Redemption rate** – The pace the protocol is currently clearing debt at,
  the same for every position in the market.
- **Borrowable** – What the cap will still let you mint.
- **Liquidation LTV** – The ratio at which the protocol can start closing part of
  the position, marked on the bar at 95%. A price move cannot carry you there.
  Choosing an LTV covers what can.
- **Liquidate** – A self-liquidation, which only you can trigger, repays the
  remaining loan from your collateral in a single transaction and leaves the
  rest of the deposit free to withdraw.

### The tool that does this for you

The vault's **Visualizer** tab takes a yield rate, a redemption rate, an alAsset
price and an external rate, and projects the deposit, the debt and the net value
across a horizon you choose. It overlays the real redemption schedule for the
near term before switching to the projection. Once you know what each input
means, it answers a sizing question faster than the arithmetic does.
