A position card in the app shows what is deposited, what is owed, and what can be
withdrawn now. There are two routes to the rest of the deposit.

The first is to withdraw whatever is already free. Any collateral the remaining
debt does not need to stay under the 90% cap can leave at any time, at no cost.

The second is to repay first. Every unit repaid frees more than one unit of
collateral, because each unit of debt holds about 1.11 units of collateral behind
it at the cap. Repay the balance in full and the whole deposit is free.

### Where the withdrawable figure comes from

A position with 10,000 deposited and 5,000 borrowed has about 4,444 available to
withdraw. The 5,000 of debt needs about 5,556 of collateral behind it at the cap,
and the rest is free.

It is the same 90% cap that limits borrowing, read the other way. When you borrow,
the cap sets the most you can take. When you withdraw, it sets the least you have
to leave.

The app shows both figures, so none of this has to be worked out by hand.

### Two details the app shows

- **Earmarked debt** – The protocol repays loans in cycles. While a cycle is under
  way, part of your loan can show as earmarked, meaning set aside for the next
  redemption. That part is repaid with MYT instead of alUSD, and the asset menu in
  the repay tab only offers what is valid. Earmarked collateral keeps earning until
  it settles.

- **Closing in one step** – A self-liquidation, which only you can trigger, repays
  the remaining loan from your collateral in one transaction and leaves the rest of
  the deposit free to withdraw.
