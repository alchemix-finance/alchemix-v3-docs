A position card shows you three numbers: what you deposited, what you owe, and how
much of the deposit you can take out today.

The amount you can take out moves two ways. Withdraw whatever is already free, which costs
you nothing. Or repay first, and every unit you repay frees more than a unit of
collateral, because each unit of debt requires about 1.11 units of collateral at
the cap. Clear the balance outright and the whole deposit unlocks.

### Where the withdrawable figure comes from

Take a position with 10,000 deposited and 5,000 borrowed. That debt needs about
5,556 of collateral behind it at the cap, which leaves about 4,444 free to
withdraw.

It is the same 90% cap that limits borrowing, read backwards. When you borrow, it
sets the most you can take out. When you withdraw, it sets the least you have to
leave in.

The app runs this arithmetic for you and shows both figures.

### What else the position card shows

- **Earmarked debt** – The protocol repays loans in cycles. While a cycle is under
  way, part of your loan shows as earmarked, meaning set aside for the next
  redemption. That part gets repaid with MYT rather than alUSD, and the asset menu
  in the repay tab offers you only what is valid. Earmarked collateral keeps earning
  until it settles.

- **Closing in one step** – A self-liquidation, which only you can trigger, repays
  the remaining loan from your collateral in a single transaction and leaves the
  rest of the deposit free to withdraw.
