- Health Factor shows how far your LTV sits from the 90% limit. It is 90% divided by your LTV, so it reads 1.80 at 50% and 1.00 at MAX LTV.
- Between 90% and 95% LTV, borrowing and withdrawing stop while the loan stays open and earning. Past 95%, anyone can liquidate the position, taking only enough deposit to bring it back to 90%.
- While you owe anything, the vault keeps Debt ÷ 0.9 of your deposit, which is why each 1 you repay frees about 1.11. The rest shows as Available on the Withdraw tab.
