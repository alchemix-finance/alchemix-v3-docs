Alchemix positions are not closed by price movement. alETH is backed by ETH and
alUSD by USDC, so collateral and debt move together and the ratio between them does
not change when the market does. A 40% drawdown in ETH leaves an ETH-denominated
position exactly where it was.

Two thresholds matter, and they do different jobs:

- **90%, the borrowing cap** – Reaching it stops further borrowing. The position
  stays open and keeps earning.
- **95%, the liquidation threshold** – Crossing it triggers a liquidation. Only the
  minimum needed to restore a healthy ratio is liquidated, and the rest of the
  position is untouched.

### What can actually move you towards it

A loss inside the Mix-Yield Token. If a strategy is exploited or reports negative
returns, the backing behind every position falls, and the liquidation marker slides towards you while your position stands still.

If backing falls by a given amount, the same debt is left standing against less
collateral. The highest starting LTV that survives is therefore the liquidation
threshold applied to whatever backing remains:

> Survivable LTV = 95% × (1 − loss)

A 12% loss leaves 83.6%. A 20% loss leaves 76%.

This is what the risk caps in lesson 2 are protecting. By limiting how much of the
vault may sit in higher risk strategies, the DAO limits how large a loss is
plausible, which is what a reasoned choice of LTV rests on.
