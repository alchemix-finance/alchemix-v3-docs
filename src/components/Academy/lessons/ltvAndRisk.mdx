- A loss inside the vault lowers your Deposit and leaves your Debt, so your LTV after it is LTV ÷ (1 − loss). From 50%, a loss of about 47% reaches 95%.
- An LTV of 36% survives the worst loss the caps allow: a 60% loss leaves 40% of the deposit, and 36 is 90% of 40.
- A liquidation settles your earmarked debt first, then takes only enough deposit to bring you back to 90%. That deposit goes to the Transmuter, and the rest of your position stays yours and keeps earning.
