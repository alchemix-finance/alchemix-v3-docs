A price move cannot close an Alchemix position. alETH is backed by ETH and alUSD
by USDC, so collateral and debt move together and the ratio between them does not
change when the market does. A 40% drawdown in ETH leaves an ETH-denominated
position exactly where it was.

The borrowing cap and the liquidation threshold do different jobs:

- **The borrowing cap sits at 90%.** Reaching it stops further borrowing. The
  position stays open and keeps earning.
- **The liquidation threshold sits at 95%.** Crossing it can close part of a
  position. Only the minimum needed to restore a healthy ratio is liquidated, and
  the rest is untouched.

### What can liquidate

A loss inside the Mix-Yield Token is the only thing that can. If a strategy is
exploited or reports negative returns, the backing behind every position falls.
The same debt now stands against less collateral, so the LTV rises.

The highest starting LTV that survives a loss is the liquidation threshold applied
to the backing that remains:

> Survivable LTV = 95% × (1 − loss)

A 12% loss leaves 83.6%. A 20% loss leaves 76%.

The risk caps inside the Mix-Yield Token bound this number. By limiting how much of
the vault may sit in higher risk strategies, the DAO limits how large a loss is
plausible. Those caps give you a worst case to size against.
