<Beside />

Say you want 5,000 dollars to spend without withdrawing your deposit. Alchemix lets you borrow up to 90% of what your deposit is worth.

Over time, Alchemix v3's redemption system pays the loan down out of your deposit.

You borrow in <Term id="alusd">alUSD</Term>, the synthetic debt token Alchemix mints when you borrow against USDC. Inside Alchemix each alUSD counts as exactly one USDC of what you owe.

In your wallet, alUSD is a token like any other: you can hold it, swap it or use it wherever it is accepted.

<ReadOn moment="read-alusd" title="What you borrow" />

<Sub>The position stats</Sub>

<There /> is the practice vault, with your 10,000 USDC deposited and nothing borrowed yet. The position stats along its top show where your loan stands.

How much you can borrow is set by your loan to value, <Term id="ltv">LTV</Term>.

<Changes>
<Change stat="debt">

<Stat name="debt">Debt</Stat> is the alUSD you owe.

</Change>
<Change stat="ltv">

<Stat name="ltv">LTV</Stat> is the share of your deposit's value that you owe: your <Stat name="debt">Debt</Stat> divided by your <Stat name="deposit">Deposit</Stat>.

</Change>
<Change stat="borrowable">

<Stat name="borrowable">Borrowable</Stat> is how much more alUSD you can borrow before your LTV reaches 90%, the <Stat name="max">MAX LTV</Stat> marker on the health bar.

</Change>
</Changes>

Alchemix lends up to 90% LTV, so against a 10,000 deposit you can borrow up to 9,000.

<ReadOn moment="read-stats" title="Debt, LTV and Borrowable" />

The <Stat name="bar">health bar</Stat> on the practice vault shows your LTV against two limits. Each limit changes what you can do with your position.

<Changes>
<Change stat="max">

<Stat name="max">MAX LTV</Stat> is 90%. Once your LTV reaches it, you can no longer borrow more or withdraw from your deposit.

</Change>
<Change stat="liq">

<Stat name="liq">LIQ LTV</Stat> is 95%. Once your LTV reaches it, part of your deposit can be used to repay your loan, which is called a liquidation.

</Change>
<Change stat="health">

<Stat name="health">Health Factor</Stat> shows how far your LTV is from MAX LTV. It reads 1.00 when your LTV is 90%, the MAX LTV, and rises as your LTV falls.

</Change>
</Changes>

<Work title="How Health Factor is worked out">

Health Factor is 90% divided by your LTV. A 95% LTV gives a Health Factor of about 0.95. With nothing borrowed it has no limit.

</Work>

<PageBreak title="Borrow 5,000" />

<Beside />

<There />, the practice vault is open on its <Stat name="tab-borrow">Borrow</Stat> tab, where you borrow <Term id="alusd">alUSD</Term> against your 10,000 deposit.

Type an amount in the <Stat name="field">alUSD field</Stat>, and the stats show your position as if you had borrowed it. Nothing is borrowed until you press <Stat name="action">Borrow</Stat>, so you can check the largest loan first.

<Do moment="max-borrow" title="Preview the largest loan">

On the <Stat name="tab-borrow">Borrow</Stat> tab, press <Stat name="max-button">MAX</Stat>, which fills the field with the most you can borrow against this deposit.

</Do>

<BorrowMoment />

<Stat name="max-button">MAX</Stat> filled the <Stat name="field">alUSD field</Stat> with 9,000, the most you can borrow against a 10,000 deposit. The previewed loan reaches the <Stat name="max">MAX LTV</Stat> marker on the health bar, so <Stat name="ltv">LTV</Stat> would read 90% and <Stat name="borrowable">Borrowable</Stat> 0.

You want 5,000 to spend. Borrowing only 5,000 keeps your LTV well below the 90% MAX LTV, so you can still borrow more or withdraw later.

<Do moment="borrow" title="Borrow 5,000 alUSD">

Clear the <Stat name="field">field</Stat>, type 5,000 and press <Stat name="action">Borrow</Stat>.

</Do>

<Await moment="borrow" hint="Type 5,000 and press Borrow" />

The vault minted 5,000 alUSD, created new for your loan, and sent it to your <Stat name="wallet">practice wallet</Stat>. Your 10,000 stays in the vault and keeps earning, because borrowing adds debt against your deposit without moving it.

<Changes>
<Change stat="debt">

<Stat name="debt">Debt</Stat> reads 5,000, one USDC of debt for each alUSD you received.

</Change>
<Change stat="ltv">

<Stat name="ltv">LTV</Stat> reads 50%: you owe 5,000 against a deposit worth 10,000, half of its value.

</Change>
<Change stat="borrowable">

<Stat name="borrowable">Borrowable</Stat> reads 4,000. Of the 9,000 you could borrow at 90%, your loan uses 5,000.

</Change>
</Changes>

<ReadOn moment="read-loan" title="What the loan changed" />

With 5,000 borrowed against 10,000, <Stat name="health">Health Factor</Stat> reads 1.80, well above the 1.00 it would read at the 90% limit.

At 90% LTV, the <Stat name="max">MAX LTV</Stat> marker, the vault stops lending and stops withdrawals. Liquidation can start only at 95%, the <Stat name="liq">LIQ LTV</Stat> marker.

Your LTV rises if you borrow more, withdraw, or a loss in the vault lowers your Deposit. At 50%, your <Stat name="ltv">LTV</Stat> has plenty of room for any of those before it reaches 90%.
