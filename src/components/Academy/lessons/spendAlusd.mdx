<Beside />

The <Term id="alusd">alUSD</Term> you borrowed is yours to hold or to swap for any other token.

Far more places take USDC, so most borrowers choose to swap their alUSD for USDC.

You can do this on any DEX that has alUSD liquidity, or trade directly from within Alchemix through the Trade page to find the best rates on your chain.

<ReadOn moment="read-swap" title="Why borrowers swap" />

<SwapExampleFigure />

<There /> is the Swap card from the app's Trade page, filled in with an example swap. You give 1,000 in the <Stat name="pay">alUSD</Stat> field and receive 970 in the <Stat name="receive">USDC</Stat> field.

The rate line reads <Stat name="price">1 alUSD = 0.97 USDC</Stat>, an example price 0.03 under a dollar. The gap between a dollar and the price alUSD sells for is called <Term id="discount">the discount</Term>.

Inside Alchemix, your debt still counts every alUSD you borrowed at a full dollar. So after this swap you owe 1,000 for 970 USDC in hand, and the gap of 30 is a cost of your loan.

<ReadOn moment="read-discount" title="The discount" />

alUSD typically trades for slightly less than a dollar, since borrowers mint it and sell it for USDC to spend.

Buyers take the other side. They deposit the alUSD they buy on the Fixed Yield page, to receive one USDC for each alUSD at the end of a set term.

Their buying pulls the price back toward a dollar.

<ReadOn moment="read-price" title="Why alUSD sells under a dollar" />

You can also receive USDC directly within the Alchemix app when you borrow. On the vault's Borrow tab, you choose which token to receive.

Choose USDC, and the app borrows alUSD and swaps it for USDC in the same transaction. The swap is routed through the DEXs on the vault's chain to find the best rate.

Your alUSD is still sold at its market price, so you pay the same discount as on a swap you make yourself.

In this lesson you make the swap yourself, so the discount shows as its own figure: the gap between the alUSD you give and the USDC you receive.

<ReadOn moment="read-swapped" title="What the swap changed" />

Most lenders charge interest for every month you owe. Alchemix charges none.

An Alchemix loan does have two costs, though. The first is the discount, 150 here, paid once if you swap your alUSD for USDC.

The second is the redemption fee. Each time a redemption pays off part of your loan out of your deposit, the protocol takes a fee on that amount.

The redemption fee is 0.25% on Ethereum, where your practice vault is. Each chain sets its own, and the [Fees page](/user/concepts/fees) in the Alchemix docs lists the redemption fee on every chain.

The practice card's rate, <Stat name="price">1 alUSD = 0.97 USDC</Stat>, is an example. The Trade page shows the real price, which changes every day.
