## How to edit this document

- Edit any text freely. Leave the numbered tags (like `3.07 · Note`) alone; they
  are how each edit finds its way back to the right place.
- Anything in {curly braces} is a number the app fills in live, because every
  learner gets different figures. You can move a placeholder within its
  sentence, but do not delete one or invent a new one.
- In the Check sections, the option marked ✓ is the correct answer. The app
  shuffles the order for every learner, so the position here does not matter,
  but each question must keep exactly four options, and the ✓ must stay on the
  same idea. The indented line under each option is the feedback shown to a
  learner who picks it.
- To cut a block, replace its text with [CUT]. To add something, write it where
  it belongs and start the line with NEW:.
- House rule carried over from the docs: no em dashes. Any that slip in will be
  rewritten with a comma or a period on the way back into the site.
- Two facts are deliberately never stated as numbers anywhere in the track: the
  live yield rate and the Transmuter term length. Both move, so the lessons make
  them sliders and point at the app. Please keep it that way.

## The story, in one page

The track makes one argument in seven steps: you can use the value of your money
without giving the money up, and the system that makes that true is safe to
touch. Each lesson runs Learn (a scenario and a guess), Try (a control panel to
push), then Check (one graded question). This is the spine to walk when you
review the flow.

**Landing page** · Promise: learn by doing. Seven short lessons, no wallet,
nothing to install, no experience assumed.

**1 · What Alchemix does** · The learner opens three product pillars, then plots
a normal loan against an Alchemix loan. Claim: the balance falls on its own, no
interest, no schedule. Leaves believing: this is not the loan I know, and the
difference is the product.

**2 · Your deposit and what it earns** · Guesses next-day availability, then
grows a deposit on a chart. Claim: deposits become MYT, the DAO steers the
strategies, there is no lock-up. Leaves believing: I know where my money is, and
I can leave whenever I want.

**3 · Borrowing against your deposit** · Guesses the borrowing cap, then pushes
a position up to it. Claim: borrow up to 90%, you receive freshly minted alUSD,
the deposit keeps earning, and the swap gap is the real cost. Leaves believing:
I know what I get, what it costs, and that hitting the cap is harmless.

**4 · The loan repays itself** · Guesses a balance after two untouched years,
then applies four events and watches which ones move it. Claim: only your own
choices move the balance; price and time do not. Leaves believing: I can leave
it alone and trust the direction.

**5 · Getting your money back** · Guesses the withdrawable amount, then repays
and watches collateral unlock. Claim: the 90% rule read backwards; every unit
repaid frees more than a unit. Leaves believing: I can get out, and the smaller
number will not surprise me.

**6 · What can go wrong** · Predicts the outcome of a 40% crash for two
borrowers, then drags a price control that does nothing and a vault-loss control
that does. Claim: price cannot liquidate, a loss inside the vault can, and the
cap and the threshold are different lines. Leaves believing: I know the one real
risk and how to size against it.

**7 · The Transmuter** · Guesses the redemption amount, then compares selling
now against waiting for par. Claim: 1:1 after a governance-set term, an early
exit costs a fee, capacity is per chain, and the guarantee is what holds the
peg. Leaves believing: I can close the loop, and the two halves are one system.

**Graduation** · The track map flips to complete, points show as banked, and the
learner is pointed at the quick start and the live app.

Two questions worth asking at each stop as you walk it:

1. Could a learner say the "leaves believing" line in their own words at that
   point? If not, the lesson taught the wrong sentence.
2. Does the lesson use a word the track has not yet earned? The order introduces
   each term before it is leaned on: MYT in lesson 2, LTV in 3, earmarked in 5,
   like-kind in 6.

  ↳ There is no fixed rate either. Alchemix loans do not accrue interest at all.

**4.10 · Headline**

**4.11 · Question 1 (graded)**
Q: Starting from an open loan you leave alone, which of these makes the amount
you owe go up?
- ✓ Borrowing more against the same position
  ↳ Right. Choosing to mint more alAssets is the only thing that raises what you owe.
- Time passing
  ↳ Time alone lowers the balance. No interest accrues, so nothing is pushing it up.
- The price of your collateral falling
  ↳ A price move does not change what you owe. Your debt is recorded in alUSD or alETH, not in dollars of collateral.
- Yield accruing in the vault
  ↳ Yield works in your favour. It never raises what you owe.

**4.12 · Question 2 (graded)**
Q: You want your loan cleared sooner than the protocol will clear it on its own.
What can you do?
- ✓ Repay some or all of it yourself, at any time
  ↳ Right. Early repayment is always available, and it unlocks your collateral immediately.
- Nothing. The schedule is fixed once the loan is open
  ↳ You are never locked in. You can repay part or all of the balance whenever you want.
- Borrow more, which speeds up the rate of repayment
  ↳ Borrowing more raises what you owe. It does not clear the loan faster.
- Withdraw collateral, which forces an early repayment
  ↳ Withdrawing collateral does not repay anything. It only moves collateral out, and only what is free to move.

**4.13 · Question 3 (graded)**
Q: You open a loan and do not touch it for a year. Which description fits what
happened to the balance?
- ✓ It fell, and it never rose at any point along the way
  ↳ Right. Left alone, the balance only moves in one direction.
- It rose steadily as interest was added
  ↳ No interest is added to an Alchemix loan, so nothing pushed the balance up.
- It stayed flat, because nothing was repaid
  ↳ It did not stay flat. The protocol was repaying it the whole time.
- It rose and fell as the market moved
  ↳ The balance does not track the market. It is recorded in alUSD or alETH and only moves as it is repaid.

### Wrap-up

**4.15 · Wrap prose**
Left alone, an Alchemix loan balance only moves in one direction. Nothing is
added for time passing, because no interest accrues, and nothing is added when
markets move.

Two things change the balance, and both are your own choices:

Borrowing more · Minting further alAssets against the same position raises what

Repaying · You can repay part or all of the balance at any time, in any amount,

Subheading: What does not change it

A fall in the price of your collateral has no effect on what you owe. Your debt
is recorded in alUSD or alETH, the same kind of asset you deposited, so a price
move changes both sides together and leaves the ratio between them alone.

Time passing has no effect beyond the decline already under way. There is no
interest, no fee for holding the loan open, and no schedule to keep.

The pace at which a balance clears depends on conditions that move, so the shape
is the lesson and any particular date will differ.
