The Academy teaches how Alchemix works by having you drive the mechanisms
yourself. Each lesson gives you a model to push on, asks you to predict what it
does before it runs, and ends with a checkpoint you have to solve rather than
recall.

It is open to everyone. There is no sign-in, no wallet connection, and no account
to create. Your progress is kept in your browser as you go.

### The track

| Lesson | What you work out |
| --- | --- |
| [The pace of repayment](./pace-of-repayment.mdx) | What decides how fast a loan clears, and what has no effect on it |
| Where the yield comes from | How collateral is put to work, and what the risk mix means |
| LTV and redemption pressure | Why a high LTV changes your exposure without a price liquidation |
| alAssets and the peg | How the Transmuter closes a gap between an alAsset and its underlying |
| Redemptions and earmarking | Where your debt sits in the queue, and why earmarked debt needs MYT |
| Capstone | Running a position through a full cycle |

Lesson 1 is available now. The rest follow.

### How the checkpoints work

Each checkpoint generates its own numbers for you, so the answer to your version
is not the answer to anyone else's. You solve it with the model on the page, and
it is marked on the server.

Completing the track earns a Discord role. Details of the reward are confirmed
before the track opens in full.

:::info Reading alongside
The Academy assumes no prior knowledge and links out to the reference docs where
you want more depth. If you would rather read first, start with
[Self-Repaying Loans](/user/concepts/self-repaying-loans).
:::
